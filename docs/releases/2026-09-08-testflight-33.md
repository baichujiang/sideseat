## Changes for testers

- Together prioritizes delivered opportunities and offers a direct Plans entry.
- Weekly Intent editing has two steps: activity, then available times. Going
  back preserves the draft; saving does not start matching automatically.
- Together and Plan surfaces share a consistent card, status and action layout.
- Plan creation and rescheduling use a native sheet with a pinned submit action.
- Accepted historical Plans show private Outcome choices. A saved answer stays
  compact and can be changed; no answer is inferred from silence.
- Shared Encounter still requires both participants to report `OCCURRED`.
